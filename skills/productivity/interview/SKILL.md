---
name: interview
description: Interview the user about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask at most 4 questions at a time — fewer is fine when the topic is narrow.

Number the questions continuously across messages so I can answer them by number.

If a question's framing depends on the answer to another, hold it until that answer is in.

If I skip some questions, treat the recommended answers as accepted for the ones I skipped, but confirm that assumption in your next message.

Don't narrate the interview mechanics — just ask the questions.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
